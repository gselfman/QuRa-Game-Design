# QuRa wardrobe v12 — final standing fit and accessory rules

The garment artwork for Ryaba (C01), Monk (C05) and Glamour (C06) moved 24 PNG pixels left inside the fixed 1200 × 1520 canvas. This removes the gap at the left-screen wing. The same main garment components update full outfits and clothing-only proofs; other sprites retain their v11 pixels.

The Russian rules define per-slot anchors, silhouette requirements, front/back splitting, independent complete clothing, footwear replacement, screen-side conventions and review combinations. Guides record actual final sprite bounds and optical anchors; bounds are reference frames, not automatic attachment points.

Validation: all 95 PNG files are transparent RGBA at 1200 × 1520; 35 equipment groups map to 77 full-outfit layers. The three corrected component instances share the same updated registration in both review modes. Both native Figma boards and the rules board were exported and visually inspected. ZIP CRC passed: 103 files, 42,850,012 bytes, SHA-256 c925f13fd7690588fd119f0edcb49db4c065ebb5af7dd62a4de6146a6973fab8.

Scope is the standing wardrobe. Perch, nest and emotion compatibility require separate QA and are not claimed complete.

Reproduction: run tools/art/wardrobe-v12/finalize_wardrobe_v12.py with QURA_V11_PACKAGE pointing to the extracted v11 package and QURA_V12_WORK_DIR containing ACCESSORY_RULES_RU.md. It applies the three registration shifts, reconstructs both review modes and writes guides. Native Figma inspection is a separate manual check.
