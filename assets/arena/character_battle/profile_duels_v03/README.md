This is a 1280×960 concept sheet, with THREE side-profile duels (3 rows × 2 characters):
- Row 1: C01 Ryaba (speckled brown, chestnut hair, straw hat, black bow, round glasses, dark brown leather) vs C02 Fighter (entirely black plumage and beak, red headband, camouflage tactical vest).
- Row 2: C03 Fool (full rainbow feathers and tail, hippie patterned scarf, pink glasses, peace jewelry) vs C04 Professor (white feathering and white messy hair, round glasses, laboratory coat, mixed green/purple clogs).
- Row 3: C05 Monk (cream plumage, conical straw hat, beads, beige robe, martial arts palm guard) vs C06 Glamour (pink feathers, pink waves, diamond jewels, dramatic eyeliner, glossy red beak, rose-gold and pink armored couture).

**Important:** All eyes, beaks, bodies face sideways into the opposing character, not towards the viewer. The aim is proper 1v1 / team wall-to-wall arena fights and more aggressive, heroically armored style. Original QuRa body coloring and iconic accessories are mandatory.

- Cut out each sprite against a genuine transparent alpha channel with feather-safe edge matting.

- Verify original character identity, style approval, and equipment compatibility; C05's third eye and C06's footwear need owner inspection.
